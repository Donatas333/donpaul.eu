01 / Overview

The question behind the project.

Flight performance data was too large and fragmented to quickly answer basic operational questions: which airports are the biggest delay contributors, which carriers underperform, and how seasonality changes delay patterns.

02 / Approach

From raw input to a useful system.

Developed a Power BI report with KPI overview, airport congestion analysis, carrier comparisons, and seasonality views — enabling fast drilldowns from headline KPIs to root causes.

  • Data Preparation
  • Modeling & Measures
  • Dashboard Build
  • Insights & Actions

03 / The outcome

What the project makes possible.

Transforms raw flight records into decision-ready KPIs — allowing stakeholders to prioritize the airports, carriers, routes, and seasons with the highest delay impact.
